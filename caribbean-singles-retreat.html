THE IDEA

A week away from the usual.

A little Caribbean sunshine. New faces. Shared stories. Time that belongs to you.

This is a carefully hosted small-group escape for single travellers who would enjoy discovering Belize alongside other independent adults. Stay close to the sea, experience the warmth and culture of Hopkins, explore beyond the coast and make space for the kind of conversations that happen when people have time to really meet.

It is not a speed-dating weekend, and there is no pressure to pair up. The invitation is simpler: come as you are, enjoy the journey, meet people naturally and let the week unfold.

YOUR HOME BY THE SEA

Seiri del Mar.
Hopkins, Belize.

For this retreat, the group will stay at Seiri del Mar, a beachfront villa property just outside Hopkins Village. Its setting by the Caribbean Sea, gardens and pool create a beautiful base for a week that balances shared experiences with time to yourself.

Villa types, room arrangements, single-occupancy options and rates will be confirmed once the dates and group requirements are checked with the property. No room-sharing arrangement will be assumed without your agreement.

A TRANSPARENT NOTE ABOUT JUNE

Caribbean weather, with room to adapt.

June marks the beginning of the rainy season in this part of Belize. Warm days are still part of the experience, but passing showers are possible and the weather can change. That is why this retreat is designed around a flexible rhythm rather than a promise of uninterrupted sunshine.

Sea-based outings will depend on marine and weather conditions. We will keep a balance of coastal time, local culture, nature, restorative experiences and open space, so the week has more than one way to be memorable.

The proposed timing is the first half of June 2027. Exact dates will be confirmed after checking accommodation availability and the group's interest. This is an expression-of-interest concept, not a confirmed departure or booking.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few things worth knowing.

Is this a dating retreat?

No. It is a hosted small-group holiday for single travellers. The focus is shared discovery, friendship and a good experience together. There is no matchmaking or pressure to form a couple.

Can I come on my own?

Absolutely. The retreat is designed for people travelling independently who would enjoy sharing part of the journey with a small group.

How many people will join?

The group will be limited to a maximum of 10 guests, keeping the experience personal and easy to navigate.

What is included in the price?

Pricing and inclusions are not published yet. Accommodation, room arrangements, activities, meals, transfers and optional experiences will be clearly listed before registration opens.
