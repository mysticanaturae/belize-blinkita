WHY 13 ZODIACS?

More than one sign. More than one description.

How often have you been told that you are a typical Pisces, Leo, Cancer or another zodiac sign?

Astrology offers different ways to look at a birth chart. Western astrology, Jyotish and the 13-zodiac system do not use the same framework, so they can offer different emphases and interpretations.

In the 13 zodiac and 13 house system, we explore all thirteen zodiac energies. Each has its place and its house. The perspective goes beyond the Sun sign to include the positions of the different energies and the areas of life associated with them.

Ophiuchus, the Serpent Bearer, is part of this wider exploration. Its presence opens an additional perspective not found in the familiar twelve-sign system.

Approximately 72 hours after payment is confirmed. During busy periods, it may take longer.

No. The book is a personal exploration of astrological symbolism and birth-chart interpretations, not a guarantee of events or a prediction of the future.
