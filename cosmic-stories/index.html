About the approach

More than a generic horoscope.

BLINKITA brings together years of personal research into astronomical cycles, Maya calendar wisdom and personal transformation.

The work combines actual astronomical constellation boundaries, traditional Maya Tzolk'in calendar knowledge, Tarot symbolism, individual interpretation and personal and family storytelling.

Each creation is approached individually. The purpose is not to define who someone must become or predict a fixed future. It is to offer another perspective for understanding oneself, appreciating relationships and consciously exploring what may lie ahead.

Frequently asked questions

A few useful details.

Are the books printed and shipped to my home?

Books are delivered digitally as PDF files prepared for printing. You can print them at home or through a professional printing service.

Can I order a book for someone else?

Yes. You can enquire about a book for children, a partner, parents, grandparents, friends or your whole family.

What if I do not know the recipient's birth time?

Share the birth date and any information you already know only after the scope is discussed privately. Exact birth time is needed only for interpretations that depend on it. The scope is confirmed before an order.

Can I order a family book for more than two people?

Yes. Family books can be created for different family sizes and configurations. Scope and price are agreed individually.

Can I keep the book a surprise?

Yes. Ask for a gift certificate so the recipient can privately provide any required birth information later.

Are readings available online?

Yes. Personal readings can be arranged online.

Can I choose which systems are included?

Yes. You can request 13-Zodiac Astrology, Maya Tzolk'in, Tarot or a combination.

How long does a book take to create?

Estimated timing is confirmed individually, based on the size and complexity of the requested creation.

Can I enquire from outside Belize?

Yes. Digital creations and online readings are available internationally.

Are the interpretations fixed predictions?

No. The readings and books offer symbolic, reflective interpretations. They do not determine a person's identity or guarantee future events.
