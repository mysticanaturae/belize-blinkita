THE ESSENCE

Living Time Reset

An immersive journey into the experience of living time.

This retreat offers a structured, experiential introduction to Living Time through the 260-day Tzolk'in cycle and BLINKITA's contemporary work with time. It is not a prediction service or a promise that life will become effortless. It is a space to study, observe, question and experience how a different relationship with time may influence the way you live.

Across twelve days, the group moves between learning, guided exploration, personal reflection and spacious time in Belize. The programme is designed as a progressive journey: from understanding the calendar's structure, through personal exploration, towards integration. The detailed daily schedule and any optional local excursions will be confirmed with participants.

BEFORE YOU BOOK

Good to know

Do I need previous experience?

No, unless a specific activity is expressly described otherwise. What matters is a willingness to participate and to approach yourself and the group with respect.

How do I secure a place?

Send an enquiry. We will check availability, answer your questions and send a written proposal detailing the programme fee, accommodation, inclusions and booking terms. A place is confirmed only after written confirmation and the agreed payment.

What if I have dietary or health considerations?

Please tell us about relevant dietary requirements, allergies, accessibility needs or other considerations before booking. We can then discuss whether the experience can be adapted safely and realistically.

Is this a medical programme?

No. This is an experiential and educational wellbeing retreat, not medical diagnosis or treatment. It does not replace advice from a doctor or other qualified healthcare professional.
