BLINKITA’s starting point · looking toward the Galactic Centre

Why does the BLINKITA circle begin in Sagittarius?

Each year around December 19, the Sun’s apparent path in the sky comes close to the direction of the Milky Way’s centre. The Sun crosses the galactic plane within about six degrees of the Galactic Centre, seen toward Sagittarius. This is an apparent alignment from Earth, not a physical meeting or closest approach. NASA · NICER on the annual apparent alignment

BLINKITA therefore opens its symbolic circle in Sagittarius. This is BLINKITA’s conscious choice: it begins with the real path of the Sun across the constellations and a remarkable December view toward the Galactic Centre. Astronomy describes the geometry; the meaning we find in it is BLINKITA’s symbolic language.
